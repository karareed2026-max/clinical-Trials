A First Look at Terminated Trials

Here are statistics on 602,514 registered studies on drug trials, with a focus on those that were marked “terminated.”

Trial status

This chart shows how many studies are in each status group. The coral bar indicates studies whose original overall_status is TERMINATED.

Trial size over time

Each panel contains one trial-level distribution for that start year. The small marks along the baseline are individual studies; the bars show how those studies are distributed. Enrollment is displayed on a logarithmic axis so that small and very large trials remain visible together. Dashed lines mark the yearly median.

Trial phase

This chart shows the percentage of studies marked terminated in each phase group throughout the duration of the drug trials observed. Small and mixed groups are also shown. No studies were removed.

Study design

This chart shows study counts by randomization and placebo reporting. The dataset keeps several study-design details instead of combining them into one score.

NoteHow to read this

These charts describe the data; they do not prove that one factor causes a trial to stop. terminated_binary marks the original TERMINATED status. We have not yet chosen a comparison group, a way to handle missing data, a disease grouping, or a complexity score.
